ABOUT US

We are Beep Boop Productions, an indie game development team based in Ireland, currently studying Game Design at TU Dublin.

As part of our final-year studies, we are working to develop and release anoriginal game called Anamir. We're bringing our individual skills, ideas and creativity to create something we're proud of.

This website is where we'll share our projects, team information, and our journey through game development.
