ABOUT US

We are Beep Boop Productions, an indie student game development team based in Ireland, currently studying Game Design at TU Dublin.

As part of our final-year studies, we are working together to develop an original game project, Anamir. We're bringing our individual skills, ideas and creativity to create something we're proud of.

This website is where we'll share our projects, our team and our journey through game development.
